# Payment acceptance marks

- visa.svg, mastercard.svg, amex.svg — from aaronfagan/svg-credit-card-payment-icons (flat-rounded set), Apache License 2.0.
- applepay.svg, applepay-white.svg — from simple-icons (CC0 1.0), recoloured.

The marks are trademarks of their owners and are shown only to tell buyers which payment methods are accepted.
Before launch, replace them with the official acceptance-mark artwork supplied in the payment provider's merchant kit
(including the official mada mark, which is not in either library) and follow each scheme's display rules.
