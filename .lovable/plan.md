# Investor Film — Insight, A–Z Platform Journey

## Production direction

- **Format:** English investor explainer, 16:9 Full HD, approximately 4–5 minutes.
- **Audience:** investors, strategic partners, licensed payment providers, and GCC enterprise buyers.
- **Style:** premium institutional finance — restrained, credible, data-led, and cinematic rather than promotional hype.
- **Visual mix:** real Insight interface walkthroughs, animated transaction-flow diagrams, selected cinematic GCC trade footage, and a final roadmap disclosure.
- **Brand:** Insight mint-teal, deep charcoal, white, and restrained gold; Manrope-led typography.
- **Narration:** confident English male voice, measured pace, with subtle cinematic corporate music beneath it.
- **Core message:** Insight coordinates the complete escrow workflow between buyer, supplier, and platform operations—from agreement and protected funding through delivery, release, refund, or dispute resolution.

## Accuracy boundaries

The film will not claim that Insight is licensed, that funds are already held by a live licensed provider, that tax invoices are ZATCA-approved, or that external arbitration is digitally integrated. It will show these as launch dependencies or operational handoffs. AI dispute analysis will be described as advisory and non-binding.

## Production steps

1. Capture clean customer and staff interface footage using safe demonstration data.
2. Build the animated transaction, dispute, arbitration, controls, and investment-case sequences.
3. Record the approved English narration and add restrained corporate music.
4. Compose captions, chapter markers, and the final brand card.
5. Export Full HD MP4, inspect key frames and transitions, verify audio levels, and provide a phone-downloadable copy.
