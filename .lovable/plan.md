# Investor film — English narrated edition

## Outcome
Produce a new professional investor film in English, using the approved 17-scene script with the user's revised first scene. The film will show the platform workflow from account creation through settlement, disputes, administration, and investor economics.

## Creative direction
- **Style:** institutional global finance; confident, precise, and credible rather than promotional hype.
- **Palette:** Insight mint `#2DD4A8`, deep charcoal `#101714`, off-white `#F4F7F5`, and restrained gold `#D2A94A` for revenue moments.
- **Typography:** bold geometric display type with a highly legible sans-serif for subtitles and interface labels.
- **Motion:** smooth camera pushes, controlled wipes, and crisp interface focus transitions; no decorative neon or cryptocurrency imagery.
- **Motifs:** protected-funds ledger, cross-border trade routes, and an expanding transaction-to-revenue flow.

## Production
1. Translate the approved Arabic narration into natural investor-grade English while preserving operational limitations and legal accuracy.
2. Record a new clear male English voiceover with measured pacing and authoritative delivery.
3. Capture a live, realistic customer journey: account access, creating a deal, setting terms, inviting the counterparty, accepting terms, funding instructions, delivery, inspection, release, and dispute actions.
4. Show the protected administration environment visually without removing two-step verification, including users, deals, risk, disputes, fee controls, currency controls, reports, and audit history.
5. Add a final investor-economics sequence showing platform-fee and FX-margin revenue flowing from transaction volume, without inventing financial projections.
6. Add concise English subtitles, restrained music, and polished transitions; export a new versioned Full HD MP4.
7. Inspect key frames and verify duration, picture, voice clarity, subtitles, and audio levels before delivery.

## Technical details
- The film will use live product captures where access permits and designed interface reenactments where protected staff screens cannot be entered safely.
- Existing security barriers remain enabled.
- Revenue is presented as a business-model mechanism, not as a forecast or guarantee.
